Welcome to Laundry Mart/ Laundry Wallah

Laundry Mart is a service-based platform that provides convenient and reliable laundry services to customers. It helps customers book laundry services and manage their clothes cleaning needs easily. The system can provide services such as washing, drying, ironing, and dry cleaning. Customers can view available services, place orders, and track their laundry status. It reduces the time and effort required to manage laundry manually. Laundry Mart also helps the business manage customers, orders, services, and payments efficiently. The main goal is to provide a simple, fast, and user-friendly laundry management experience.
